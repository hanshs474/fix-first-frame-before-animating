Fix the First Frame Before You Animate a Photo

An image-to-video model animates whatever is in the still, including the clutter and the bad light. Fix the frame here first, then use it as the opening shot of the clip.

How to get a clean result

  1. Upload the photo you want to animate and fix what would distract in motion: stray objects, harsh shadows, a crooked horizon.
  2. Keep the subject untouched, so the clip starts from the face or product you actually shot.
  3. Download the edited still and upload it as the first frame in an image-to-video model.

Prompts that work

Remove the clutter from the table and make the lighting soft and even. Keep the person unchanged.

Straighten the horizon and remove the tourists in the background. Keep the couple unchanged.

Make the sky clear blue and remove lens flare. Keep the car exactly as it is.
